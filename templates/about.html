NIST SP 800-63B Guidelines & Password Awareness

  • Length Over Complexity: A 16-character passphrase like Correct-Horse-Battery-Staple is far harder to crack and easier to remember than P@ssw0rd!.
  • Block Compromised Passwords: Always check passwords against known breach corpuses (like HIBP) rather than forcing arbitrary 90-day password rotations.
  • Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Even a strong password can be phished. Always enable TOTP authenticator apps or FIDO2 security keys.
  • Use a Password Manager: Never reuse passwords across websites. Let a reputable manager generate and store unique credentials.
